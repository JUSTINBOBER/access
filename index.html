why in-home pet sitting?

Home is where your pet is happiest

Kennels and boarding facilities can be loud, unfamiliar and stressful, especially for older pets, anxious dogs and cats who don't like change. With in-home pet sitting from Paws & Play, your dog or cat stays right where they feel safest, with their own bed, their own toys and their usual routine. I come to you, so there's no packing, no drop-off and no exposure to other animals.

The comfort of their own home

Familiar smells, favorite napping spots and a quiet house mean less stress for your pet while you travel or work long days.

Their routine, unchanged

Meals, walks, playtime and bedtime happen the way your pet is used to, following your instructions.

Peace of mind for you

A dependable, animal-loving sitter checks on your pets and your home, so you can relax while you're away.

Pet sitting FAQ

What is in-home pet sitting?

In-home pet sitting means your dog or cat stays in their own home while a trusted sitter comes to them. Pets keep their routine, their own bed and their favorite spots, which is far less stressful than a kennel or boarding facility.

How much does pet sitting cost?

Drop-in visits for feeding, playtime and cuddles are $60. Day visits are $85. Overnight stays are $100. Dog walks can be added for $5.

Do you pet sit cats as well as dogs?

Yes. Paws & Play Pet Sitting cares for both dogs and cats. Cats in particular do best at home, where their territory and litter routine stay the same.

Can we meet before booking?

Absolutely. Call (330) 690-0090 or email pawsandplaypetsoh@gmail.com to schedule an interview or meet and greet so your pets can get to know their sitter and you can go over routines, feeding and house instructions.

What should I leave out for the pet sitter?

Please leave your pet's food, treats, leash and any care instructions, and please provide cleaning products for accidents. Additional charges may apply if cleaning materials are not available.

What areas do you serve?

Paws & Play Pet Sitting serves families across Northeast Ohio. Call (330) 690-0090 or email pawsandplaypetsoh@gmail.com to check availability in your town.
